- Scrolling: off macOS, a trackpad swipe now throws its scroll view when the fingers lift, as Chrome on Linux does (Chromium's sources at 2026-11-17). The release speed is fitted by least squares to the last three scroll frames, timed by the platform's own event times, and nothing throws when the lift comes more than 200 ms after the last movement. The view travels v² / 2a (a = 2,645.7 points per s², at most three window extents) on Chromium's `PhysicsBasedFlingCurve`. Fingers resting on the touchpad (Wayland's `zwp_pointer_gesture_hold_v1`, now `WindowEvent::HoldGesture` in the vendored winit, handed to `Runtime::touchpad_hold`) or a new swipe stop it where it is. A swipe that begins within 50 ms of that stop and heads the same way adds the stopped fling's speed, as Chromium's fling booster does. Horizontal scrollers throw too. `WheelEvent::time` carries the event's platform time (`WindowEvent::MouseWheel::time` on Wayland).
