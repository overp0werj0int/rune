- **The first frame that fades, clips or turns a group no longer waits for the isolated layers' pipelines.** A native renderer makes them on a thread of its own from when it is made, checked in that thread's error scopes, and the first frame that needs them takes them (waiting if they aren't done), so it draws the same picture. On a Vulkan laptop the frame that starts a note's cross-fade went from 53 ms to 15 ms; the pipelines had cost it 40 ms. A shader change starts them again.
