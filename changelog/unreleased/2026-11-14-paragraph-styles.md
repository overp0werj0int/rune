- **A multi-line text field takes a `ParagraphStyle` per paragraph with `El::paragraph_styles`: an indent, a first line that starts early or late or hangs its first bytes before the indent, its own line height, and room above it.** A Markdown source editor hangs `# `, `- ` and `> ` in a gutter with `ParagraphStyle::new().indent(48.0).hang(2)`: the mark stays text the caret steps through, a selection covers and a press lands on, the words after it start at the indent, and so do the paragraph's wrapped lines. Rune measures the hung bytes as it shaped them, so the app measures nothing. `line_height` sets every line of the paragraph, whatever sizes its spans set, so a heading's bigger span no longer takes the body's line-height ratio; `space_above` adds room above its first line, which a press in gives to that paragraph. The indent is from the paragraph's start edge, the right of a right-to-left one, and an empty paragraph's caret stands at it. The styles are layout, passed on every build like `El::spans`: they're no part of `TextEdit` or its undo history, and a preedit typed inside hung bytes hangs with them. A field with styles lays out by paragraph (`El::dir_by_paragraph`), so an edit shapes only the paragraph it changes; a paragraph with hung bytes breaks its lines twice when it's shaped or the width changes. A one-line, secure or line-clamped field and static text ignore them.
